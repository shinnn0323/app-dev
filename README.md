## About the Band
The 1975 is an English pop rock band formed in Wilmslow, Cheshire. Known for their eclectic sound and thought-provoking lyrics, they span genres from indie pop and electronic rock to synth-pop.

### Notable Members
* **Matty Healy** (Lead Vocals, Rhythm Guitar)
* **Adam Hann** (Lead Guitar)
* **George Daniel** (Drums, Production)
* **Ross MacDonald** (Bass)
